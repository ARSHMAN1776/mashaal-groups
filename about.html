OUR JOURNEY

Three Decades of Enduring Progress

From foundational regional trading to an integrated conglomerate spanning four vital global industries.

1998
Foundations in Energy & Fuel Supply
Commenced operations as a regional fuel distribution and bulk terminal logistics provider, laying the cornerstone for Mashal Petroleum.
2006
Expansion into Maritime Freight
Incorporated Mashwani Shipping L.L.C. to support intercontinental bulk grain and energy corridors with direct vessel charters.
2014
Mashal Foods & Cold-Chain Integration
Inaugurated dedicated milling, spice processing, and temperature-controlled food distribution complexes across 12 countries.
2020
Prestige Mobility & Fleet Electrification
Launched Mashal Rent A Car's corporate mobility division, scaling to 1,200+ executive vehicles and zero-emission luxury EV fleets.
2026
Unified Holding & ESG Framework
Consolidated under Mashal Groups Holding structure with cross-sector synergy, institutional governance, and green transition leadership.
